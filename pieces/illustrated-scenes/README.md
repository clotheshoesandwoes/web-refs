# Illustrated scenes

For The Grid: painted, gently animated scenes of real North County and La Jolla coast spots, the way macOS names its wallpapers after California places, but our own places. Started from two footers Sean saved on X (Nov 2026).

## The two refs

<img src="img/pinverst-footer.png" width="100%">

**Pinverst footer (Muddasir, X).** Style: **anime background art**, the painted-sky look of Makoto Shinkai and Studio Ghibli backgrounds. What makes it: a sky that does most of the work (pink-gold cumulus, a low sun with a light path on the water), warm rock against cool sea, crisp white foam, and soft edges everywhere except the silhouettes. The motion is small and from the subject: gulls crossing, waves rolling in. The painting fades to white into the page, so the footer text sits on clean paper.

<img src="img/sequel-footer.jpg" width="100%">

**Sequel footer (Pranav, X).** Style: **vintage engraving** (line or stipple etching), printed two-tone: cobalt on cream, like a cyanotype or blue toile. What makes it: one ink color, texture made of lines instead of shading, a tiny figure for scale, an empty sky, and serif type in the same blue so the text and picture read as one print.

*Ponyo, Kiki.* A bright seaside town: crayon-soft greens, a house on the cliff, the harbor full of small boats, cumulus stacked high. This is the closest match to a sunny Encinitas or La Jolla day.

**Rules taken from these:** flat shapes with soft painted edges, sky first, warm land against cool water, one light source with long shadows, small things moving (gulls, a wave line, a flag, a cloud drifting), no outlines on landscape.

**Japanese woodblock prints (ukiyo-e): Hokusai, Hiroshige.** Flat color areas, Prussian-blue water, bold outlines on waves, rain drawn as lines. The bridge between engraving and anime; a good fit for the ocean.

<p>
<img src="img/wpa-see-america.jpg" width="32%"> <img src="img/wpa-park-1.jpg" width="32%">
</p>

**1930s WPA national park posters.** Screen-printed, 4-6 flat colors, huge simple shapes, sunset gradients in bands. Very "California coast," and easy to make in code (SVG layers).

<p>
<img src="img/homer-palm-nassau.jpg" width="49%"> <img src="img/homer-gulf-stream.jpg" width="49%">
</p>

**Winslow Homer's watercolors.** Tropical water and palms in loose transparent washes. The most natural look for a beach scene.

## Making a scene

1. Paint the still: an image model given the place photo plus 2-3 of the refs above, in "anime background art" style, no people in front.
2. Split it into layers (sky, far land, sea, near rocks, foreground) so each can move a little.
3. Animate in code from the subject only: gulls on a path, a wave line sliding in, clouds drifting, sun glints flickering. One loop, seamless, under 10 seconds.
4. Check it on a phone at night and at noon, next to the ref, before it ships.
